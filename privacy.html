Privacy Policy

Effective Date: November 2026  |  Publisher: Ultron Systems

100% On-Device Architecture: Textoo does not maintain remote cloud servers or third-party databases. All scheduled routines, recipient numbers, and message bodies are stored locally in your phone's encrypted private Room database.

1. Overview & Purpose

This Privacy Policy describes how Ultron Systems ("we", "us", "our") handles data within the Textoo Android application. Textoo is designed specifically to help users maintain medication adherence, establish healthy routines, and foster authentic personal connections with family and loved ones through scheduled SMS dispatches.

2. Sensitive Permissions & Declarations

To execute its core functionality without requiring users to manually press "Send" at inconvenient times (e.g. while driving, working, or sleeping), Textoo requests the following specific Android permissions:

  • SEND_SMS (Core Functional Purpose): Enables Textoo to dispatch user-scheduled messages via the cellular network. The application does not read external incoming SMS messages, does not monitor incoming conversations, and only sends messages specifically authored and scheduled by the user.
  • READ_CONTACTS (Address Book Picker): Used exclusively to let you choose recipients from your existing device contact book with one tap. Contacts are never uploaded or synced to external servers.
  • SCHEDULE_EXACT_ALARM: Necessary to ensure that time-critical medication doses and health reminders fire with minute-level precision, uninhibited by Android Doze power-saving states.
  • READ_PHONE_STATE: Enables multi-SIM detection so you can specify whether an automation should be routed via SIM 1 or SIM 2.

3. Advertising via Google AdMob

Textoo integrates the Google Mobile Ads (AdMob) SDK to display banner ads, native video cards, and opt-in rewarded video ads to unlock cosmetic dark themes.

Google AdMob may collect and process anonymous device telemetry (such as your Android Advertising ID, general device type, and country/city location) to deliver personalized or non-personalized advertisements according to Google's Ad Policies:
https://policies.google.com/technologies/ads

4. Data Retention & Deletion

Because all data is stored strictly on your local device, you retain absolute control over your records. You can delete individual automations or history logs at any time from within the app, or delete all application data through your Android system settings.
