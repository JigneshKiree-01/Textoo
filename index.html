Deliverability Architecture

Why Carrier SMS Over Other Messaging Apps?

Modern instant messaging platforms are noisy, easily muted, and critically dependent on active internet connections.

Other Messaging Apps

  • ✕ Requires Active Internet: If a recipient turns off mobile data, has low signal, or runs out of bandwidth, critical time-sensitive alerts are delayed or missed entirely.
  • ✕ Lost in Continuous Clutter: Vital personal notices get buried under high-volume group chats, forwarded media, and promotional notifications.
  • ✕ Recipient Software Dependencies: Requires the other person to have an active account, compatible phone hardware, and the exact same proprietary app installed.

Textoo Native Carrier SMS

  • ✓ 100% Guaranteed Deliverability: Dispatches via universal cellular networks directly to any handset—from modern flagship smartphones to basic feature phones.
  • ✓ Commands Immediate Priority: The native cellular SMS inbox is clean, personal, and universally recognized for urgent, high-priority notifications.
  • ✓ Zero Recipient Setup: Your recipients do not need to install any app, create accounts, or learn any new software.
Precision Architecture

The Textoo Engine Under the Hood

Engineered with Android's highest standards of battery optimization, memory efficiency, and offline resilience.

01

Exact Alarm Scheduler

Bypasses standard Android Doze batching delays using high-precision AlarmManager APIs, ensuring your time-critical alarms trigger to the exact minute.

02

Dual-SIM Carrier Router

Directly interfaces with Android Telephony SubscriptionManager, letting you bind specific automations to SIM 1 or SIM 2 dynamically.

03

100% On-Device Room DB

Zero external API dependencies. All messages, schedules, and recipient logs are stored locally in an encrypted SQLite Room database on your physical device.

04

Reboot Resilience

Integrated BootReceiver automatically reschedules all active recurring alarms the instant your phone powers on, ensuring no automations are ever dropped.
