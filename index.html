Deliverability Engineering

Why Carrier SMS Over WhatsApp?

Instant messaging platforms are noisy, frequently muted, and critically depend on Wi-Fi or active mobile data.

Chat Apps (WhatsApp / Telegram)

  • ✕ Requires Internet: If an elderly family member turns off mobile data or enters a low-coverage zone, critical medication alerts are missed.
  • ✕ Buried in Noise: Important check-ins get lost under group forward messages, status updates, and notifications.
  • ✕ Recipient Overhead: The other person must have a smartphone, active account, and compatible software version installed.

Textoo Native SMS Engine

  • ✓ 100% Guaranteed Deliverability: Transmits via cellular towers directly to any handset — modern smartphone or basic keypad feature phone.
  • ✓ High-Priority Attention: The native SMS inbox is clean, personal, and universally recognized for vital, urgent dispatches.
  • ✓ Zero Recipient Setup: Your loved ones do not need to install any app or learn any new technology.
Precision Architecture

The Textoo Engine Under the Hood

Engineered with Android's highest standards of battery optimization, memory efficiency, and offline resilience.

01

Exact Alarm Scheduler

Bypasses standard Android Doze batching delays using high-precision AlarmManager APIs, ensuring your time-critical medicine alarms trigger to the exact minute.

02

Dual-SIM Carrier Router

Directly interfaces with Android Telephony SubscriptionManager, letting you bind specific automations to SIM 1 or SIM 2 dynamically.

03

100% On-Device Room DB

Zero external API dependencies. All messages, schedules, and recipient logs are stored locally in an encrypted SQLite Room database on your physical device.

04

Reboot Resilience

Integrated BootReceiver automatically reschedules all active recurring alarms the instant your phone powers on, ensuring no automations are ever dropped.
